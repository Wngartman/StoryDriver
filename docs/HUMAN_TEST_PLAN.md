# Human Acceptance

Automated tests and screenshots are not a substitute for these checks on your own hardware.

1. Install in chosen folders, open the app, and confirm no console windows appear.
2. Add a GGUF in Settings > Writing, load/test it, then generate and continue a story.
3. Rewrite, revise and regenerate a selected scene; check version navigation and unchanged scene count.
4. Edit the system prompt and task notes, close settings immediately, reopen and verify exact text.
5. Review introductions, scope, time, character agency, object ownership, relationships and viewpoint.
6. Listen to a long chapter. Judge voice naturalness, pronunciation, emotion and fatigue.
7. Pause/resume, skip, change speed, refresh the page and reconnect narration after a provider restart.
8. On a physical phone, check LAN access, portrait/landscape, typing, safe areas, lock screen and background audio.
9. Back up, export, restore a disposable story, and verify an upgrade/uninstall preserves the data folder.
10. Compare resource usage with your writing model loaded and while narration is generating.

Expected limits: local model quality varies; strict word counts may drift even after one repair.
Long chapters can exceed two minutes. Browser speech fallback depends on OS/browser voices.
Unsigned installers may trigger SmartScreen. LAN has no authentication and is trusted-network-only.
